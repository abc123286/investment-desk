    Signal record

    A signal is issued when a stock's Balanced label reaches Accumulate or better and its plan has at least 1.5 : 1 reward/risk. Its entry, stop and target are locked at that moment and never move. It closes when the price hits the stop or the target, when the label falls to Watchlist or lower ("withdrawn"), or after a year ("expired"). After a stop-out the stock gets no new signal for 10 trading days. Results assume a buy at the entry midpoint and are checked on the 15-minute delayed prices, so a real order could fill differently.

    News intelligence

    Each event appears once, however many outlets covered it. Its −100 to +100 impact score is an AI judgement (Claude's reading of the linked reports). A stock's news score is then calculated in code from the events tagged to it, weighted by how directly each one hits it and by how recent it is.

    Method, data status and sources

    What is calculated, and what is judged

    Calculated by one engine file (engine.js) that both this page and the update job run, so they always agree: every sub-score, the overall score, data confidence, labels, market moods and the headline, the trade plan and position size, and each stock's net news score.

    Judged by AI (Claude, in the scheduled news sweeps): the −100…+100 impact score of each news event, how directly it hits each tagged stock (relevance 0.2–1), and the catalyst and risk notes of researched stocks. Each note shows the date it was written; numbers inside it are as of that date.

    Sub-scores

    All on fixed scales, so adding or removing other stocks never changes a stock's score.

    • Valuation: forward earnings yield (1 ÷ forward P/E at today's price). 2% = 0, 12% = 100.
    • Analyst upside: consensus target ÷ price − 1. 0% = 0, 40%+ = 100. Left out when fewer than 5 analysts cover the stock or there is no consensus rating.
    • Growth: implied next-year EPS growth (trailing P/E ÷ forward P/E − 1). −5% = 0, +35% = 100. Below −25% or above +60% is marked unreliable and left out.
    • Momentum: 20-trading-day return (−20% = 0, +20% = 100), or position in the 52-week range when there's no price history ("proxy").
    • Pullback (Value strategy): 100 − momentum.
    • Income: dividend yield. US: 4%+ = 100. Bursa: 6%+ = 100 (Malaysian yields are normally higher).
    • Stability: annualised 20-day volatility (15% = 100, 80% = 0), or 52-week range ÷ price ("proxy").
    • News: sum of (event score × relevance × recency), recency halving every 14 days, capped at ±100, mapped to 0–100.

    Missing inputs count as 0. So a missing forward P/E or target can only lower a score, never raise it. If 30% or more of a strategy's weight is missing, the stock shows Incomplete data instead of a label.

    Labels

    70+ Strong Buy Candidate (needs confidence 75+), 62+ Buy Candidate, 55+ Accumulate, 47+ Hold, 40+ Watchlist, 33+ Reduce, below 33 Avoid. These bands are judgement and not backtested (see Signal record).

    Caps: Hold at most for stocks nobody has researched yet. Watchlist at most when the price misses more than 3 closes, confidence is below 60, a recent event scores −60 or worse for the stock, an event risk is open, the locked stop has been hit, or within 10 trading days of a stop-out. Event risks stay open until a person marks them resolved; they never expire on their own.

    Data confidence

    Separate from attractiveness. Starts at 100: −15 for a partial-day price (fetched before a close that has now passed); −8 per newer close that's missing; −10 for a single price source; −10 if valuation inputs are over 30 days old or their date is unknown (Yahoo); −10 if two sources disagree by over 25% on forward P/E (the less flattering one is used); −15 for no forward P/E; −15 if analyst upside is left out; −15 if not researched; −5 each for fewer than 10 analysts, unknown dividend, proxy momentum, unreliable growth, or a price outside the 52-week range on file; −10 for US stocks under Growth & momentum while the US mood is high-volatility.

    Trade plan and locked signals

    Stop distance = 1.5 × daily volatility × √10 (about a 10-day move), kept between 5% and 15%; without price history, 20% of 52-week range ÷ price, kept between 6% and 15%. Entry zone = price down to price × (1 − stop distance ÷ 3). Stop = entry midpoint × (1 − stop distance). Prices snap to Bursa tick sizes (0.5 sen under RM1, 1 sen to RM9.99, 2 sen to RM99.98, 10 sen above) or US cents. Below 1.5 : 1 reward/risk the plan is marked not worth taking. Position size = (account × risk %) ÷ (entry − stop), in 100-share lots for Bursa.

    While no signal is open, the plan is indicative and recalculated each update. Once a signal is issued (Balanced label Accumulate or better, plan worth taking), its entry, stop and target are locked and never follow the price. See Signal record.

    Where the data comes from

    Every 15 minutes in each market's trading hours, a GitHub job fetches prices, 52-week ranges, P/E, targets and 30 days of closes from Yahoo Finance (about 15 minutes delayed) with the open-source yfinance tool, then locks or closes signals. Exchange holidays and US half-days are built in. If fewer than half the stocks of a market update, the job is marked failed, older prices are kept and labelled, and a banner says so. Researched valuation figures are kept while under 30 days old, otherwise Yahoo's are used. Company codes are checked against Yahoo's company name. US live charts come from TradingView, which has no Bursa data in its free widgets.

    Not included

    MACD, moving averages, volume indicators, candlestick patterns, ROE/margins, DCF valuations and a historical backtest.

    Price status legend

    • Intraday: fetched while the market is open; about 15 minutes delayed.
    • Close: that day's closing price, and no newer close is missing.
    • Partial day: fetched before a close that has since passed. It is not a closing price (−15 confidence).
    • Old: one or more newer closes are missing (−8 each; after 3, capped at Watchlist).
    • Times are shown in Malaysia time (MYT). Closes: 5pm Kuala Lumpur; 4pm New York (1pm on US half-days).
