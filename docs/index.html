    Method, data status and sources

    What is calculated, and what is judged

    Calculated in this page's code (deterministic, from the sourced figures): every sub-score, the overall score, data confidence, labels, market-mood labels, the trade plan and position size, and each stock's net news score.

    Judged by AI (Claude, when the snapshot was built): the −100…+100 impact score of each news event, how directly each event hits each tagged stock (relevance 0.2–1), catalyst and risk wording. These are labelled where they appear.

    Sub-scores

    Valuation, analyst upside, income and stability blend an absolute scale (50%) with the stock's rank within its own market (50%), so US and Bursa stocks are not judged on one scale. Sector-relative ranking needs a bigger universe than two or three peers per sector, so it is not used yet.

    • Valuation: forward earnings yield (1 ÷ forward P/E at today's price). 2% = 0, 12% = 100.
    • Analyst upside: consensus target ÷ price − 1. 0% = 0, 40%+ = 100.
    • Growth: implied next-year EPS growth (trailing P/E ÷ forward P/E − 1). −5% = 0, +35% = 100. Values below −25% or above +60% usually reflect one-off gains or accounting differences, so they are marked unreliable and left out of the score.
    • Momentum: 20-trading-day price return where 30 days of closes were available (−20% = 0, +20% = 100). Otherwise, position in the 52-week range, marked "proxy".
    • Pullback (Value strategy): 100 − momentum.
    • Income: dividend yield; 6%+ = 100.
    • Stability: annualised volatility of daily returns over 20 days (15% = 100, 80% = 0). Otherwise, 52-week range ÷ price, marked "proxy".
    • News: sum of (event score × relevance × recency), recency halving every 14 days, capped at ±100, then mapped to 0–100.

    Any sub-score that can't be calculated is left out and the remaining weights are scaled up to 100%. If less than half the strategy's weight can be calculated, the stock gets no score.

    Labels

    Scores are rounded to whole numbers, then labelled: 70+ Strong Buy Candidate (needs confidence 75+), 62+ Buy Candidate, 55+ Accumulate, 47+ Hold, 40+ Watchlist, 33+ Reduce, below 33 Avoid. 50 is neutral. These bands are judgement, not backtested; backtesting needs a price-history feed this page doesn't have.

    The label is capped at Watchlist when the price is more than five trading days old, data confidence is below 60, a recent event scores −60 or worse for the stock, or an unresolved event risk is flagged.

    Data confidence

    Separate from attractiveness. Starts at 100: −6 per trading day of price age beyond two; −10 if the price has only one source; −10 if valuation inputs are over 30 days old; −15 if forward P/E or target is missing; −5 each for proxy momentum/volatility, unreliable growth, a price outside its stored 52-week range, or fewer than 10 analysts; −10 for US stocks under Growth & momentum while the US mood is high-volatility.

    Trade plan

    Stop distance = 1.5 × daily volatility × √10 (about a 10-day move), kept between 5% and 15%; without price history, 20% of 52-week range ÷ price, kept between 6% and 15%. Entry zone = price down to price × (1 − stop distance ÷ 3). Stop = entry midpoint × (1 − stop distance). Reward/risk is measured from the entry midpoint. Prices snap to Bursa tick sizes (0.5 sen under RM1, 1 sen to RM9.99, 2 sen to RM99.98, 10 sen above) or US cents. Below 1.5 : 1 reward/risk the plan is shown but marked not worth taking. Position size = (account × risk %) ÷ (entry − stop), in 100-share board lots for Bursa.

    Website version: automatic data

    On the website, prices, 52-week ranges, P/E, analyst targets and 30-day closes for every scored stock are refreshed every 15 minutes during each market's trading hours from Yahoo Finance (prices about 15 minutes delayed) (through the open-source yfinance tool, run by a scheduled GitHub Action). That is one source, so each price loses 10 confidence points; if Yahoo lacks a forward P/E or target, that part is left out. Company codes are checked against Yahoo's company name so a wrong code can't show the wrong company. US live charts and the whole-US-market screener come from TradingView widgets; TradingView's free widgets carry no Bursa data, so Bursa uses the price board. News scores still come from the researched event list.

    Not in this snapshot

    MACD, moving averages, volume indicators, candlestick patterns, ROE/margins, DCF valuations and backtests. Momentum, volatility and RSI are calculated only for the 11 stocks with 30 days of closes.

    Data status legend

    • EOD: end-of-day close, two trading days old or less.
    • Stale: older than two trading days. Confidence drops 6 points a day; after five days the label is capped at Watchlist.
    • 2 sources / 1 source: whether the closing price was confirmed by a second independent page.
    • Ages are measured from each market's close (4pm New York, 5pm Kuala Lumpur) to your device's clock now.
